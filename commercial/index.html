Restaurant Services

Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Few businesses work a water heater harder than a restaurant. Prep sinks, pre-rinse sprayers, and dish cycles all draw from the same tank, a lunch rush can outrun a small heater quickly, and the dish machine depends on a booster to reach its final rinse temperature. South Lyon Water Heater sizes, repairs, and maintains the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep both the kitchen and the restrooms supplied, and we schedule visits around your hours.

Temperature is where many restaurant hot water problems start. When the main heater can't keep up, handwashing sinks run lukewarm, the three-compartment sink falls below temperature, and the dish machine struggles to sanitize at peak service. We verify temperatures from the tank all the way to the fixtures and log every visit in written reports that inspectors and landlords can review.

Your schedule comes first. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and downtime stays short so the kitchen opens on time.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, Legionella risk in tanks stored too cool, and expensive water damage when a tank fails. That's a real liability for any commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Our commercial installations and inspections cover venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test dates, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.

Emergency Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

Each hour without hot water costs a business money. In a restaurant, it can bring the dish room to a halt. In a salon, it cancels appointments. In a hotel, it means refund requests, and in a multifamily building it invites tenant disputes. South Lyon Water Heater offers fast emergency water heater repair for commercial properties across the area, and we can schedule planned work after hours to keep downtime short.

Failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, stalled recirculation pumps, tankless lockouts: our technicians handle all of it on tank, tankless, and boiler-fed systems. Our first priority is diagnosing the fault and restoring hot water as quickly as possible, often by isolating the failed heater so the others carry the load. After that, we put maintenance in place to prevent a repeat, and each response is fully documented for your facility files.

Ongoing Protection

Proactive Water Heater Care for Businesses

Our commercial maintenance program is built around your property's specific equipment, how the building uses hot water, and your budget. On each scheduled visit, we handle anode checks and tank flushing, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner, flue, and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve service, and temperature verification at the tap. Hard water shortens the life of commercial equipment quickly, so high-use properties usually benefit from more frequent visits.

Next to an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and failed inspections. Businesses that commit to a program typically see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower fuel bills. Give us a call to discuss a plan for your property and budget.
